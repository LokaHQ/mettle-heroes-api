# Customer questions and answers

For Customer Support. Use these answers when replying to clients and members of the public.
Last updated: 12 January 2026

## Plans and billing

### What plans do you offer?
We offer three plans, priced per patrol post per month. Pro is $8,000 a post, Max is $16,000 and Enterprise is $28,000. Every plan includes a set number of hero-hours per post.

### What is a seat?
A seat is one patrol post: a hero slot reserved for you every month, staffed around the clock by our field crews, with a signed hero when you book one.

### Can we change plan in the middle of the month?
Yes. Changes take effect on the day you ask, and the invoice is adjusted for the days on each plan.

### When are invoices sent?
On the first working day of each month, for the month before. Invoices go to the billing contact on your account.

### We used all our hero-hours. What happens now?
Bookings beyond your included hours are not accepted. Your account director can move you to a larger plan or add posts.

## Support and response times

### How fast will you reply to a ticket?
Enterprise clients get a first reply within 1 hour. Max clients get a first reply within 12 hours, and Pro clients within 24 hours.

### How do we reach you outside office hours?
Enterprise clients can call the dispatch line at any time. Everyone else can raise a ticket in the booking app, and we reply within the target for their plan.

### A hero arrived late. Can we get a refund?
If a hero arrives more than 30 minutes after the booked start, we refund the hero-hours for that booking. Raise a ticket with the booking reference.

## Damage and complaints

### A hero damaged our property. What do we do?
Raise a ticket with photos, the date, the time and the address. A damage claims manager will contact you, usually within two working days, to arrange an assessment. Mettle & Co pays for repairs to damage caused during a booked mission.

### I'm a member of the public and a hero damaged my property. Can I claim?
Yes. Use the public claims form on our website or contact the council that booked the patrol. We handle the claim the same way as a client claim.

### A hero was rude or unhelpful. Who do I tell?
Raise a ticket under hero conduct. The Director of Dispatch reviews every conduct ticket.

### Can we ask for a specific hero?
Enterprise clients have a named lead hero. Other clients can ask for a hero when booking, and we try to send them if they are free.

## Accounts and the booking app

### How do we add a new user to the booking app?
Your account admin can add users under Settings, then Users. If your admin has left, raise a ticket and we'll reset admin access after checking with your billing contact.

### Can we book the same slot every week?
Not yet. Each booking has to be made on its own. Recurring bookings are on our list.

### Where is your office?
Our head office is the Old Mill office on Tanner Street, Brindlemouth. Visitors should sign in at the front desk.
