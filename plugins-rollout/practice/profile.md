# Mettle & Co company profile

Mettle & Co signs superheroes, manages their public image and sells their services to councils and companies across Carrowmere. Most of the work is ordinary: patrol cover for a town centre, a hero at a store opening, someone to stop the queue at the ferry terminal from getting out of hand. The company is based in Brindlemouth, the largest city in Carrowmere.

Clients rent patrol posts. A post is one reserved hero slot, staffed around the clock by the field crews and topped up with a signed hero when the client books one. The field crews do the routine work. The 15 signed heroes do the visible work, and they are the reason most clients sign.

## Plans and prices

Clients sign on one of three plans. Prices are per seat per month, and a seat is one patrol post.

| Plan | Price per seat | Hero-hours per seat | Dispatch hours | First reply to support tickets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $9,000 | 160 | 8am to 6pm, weekdays | within 24 hours |
| Max | $16,000 | 250 | 7am to 11pm, every day | within 8 hours |
| Enterprise | $28,000 | 400 | 24 hours, every day | within 1 hour |

Enterprise clients also get a named lead hero and a quarterly review with their account director. Some contracts carry a negotiated discount of 10 or 15 percent, so a client's monthly value is not always seats times list price.

All clients moved to these three plans on 1 January 2026. A client's start date is the start of its current contract.

## Locations

Head office is the Lighthouse Quay office in Brindlemouth. The company moved there from the Old Mill office on Tanner Street in December. Dispatch, the field crews and Customer Support work from the Eastgate depot. About a fifth of office staff work remotely.

## Rules people ask about

- Receipts are needed for any expense over $50.
- Property damage caused on a mission is claimed through Customer Support, not paid by the hero.
- First-reply targets for support tickets are set by plan, as shown in the table above.
