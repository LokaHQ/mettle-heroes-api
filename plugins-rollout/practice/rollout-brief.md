# Rollout brief: Support desk plugin for Customer Support

From Luis Ferreira, VP Customer Support, to the Claude Owner and the champions. Mettle & Co, December 2026.

## What we're rolling out

A Support desk plugin: weekly ticket triage against first-reply targets, reply drafting from the FAQ, and call follow-up. It's tested and ready to publish.

## The team

- 369 people in Customer Support, on two shifts led by Ravi Chandran and Chloe Dubois. 90 of them have Claude seats.
- Only about a third of those 90 use Claude in a typical week.
- Our champion, Rui, moved to Operations last month, and nobody has replaced him yet.
- Chloe Dubois has offered to be the plugin's owner. Two agents on Ravi's shift use Claude every day and could help as champions.

## Constraints

- We're on Claude Enterprise. There's a Customer Support group in the organisation settings.
- Zendesk hasn't been added as a Claude connector, so the team exports tickets to CSV every Monday morning. The weekly triage has to work from that file.
- Monday 9:30 is the stand-up. The triage must be ready by then.
- Nobody on the team should have to install anything themselves.
- Client names and call notes stay inside Customer Support.

## What I want to see

- Within a month: both team leads use the weekly triage every Monday, and no Enterprise miss goes unnoticed for more than a week.
- Within a quarter: most of the team uses reply drafting, and the FAQ is fixed.
- A named owner who keeps the plugin up to date.
