# Customer Support: how the team works

Written by Ravi Chandran, Support Team Lead, for the Claude champions. Mettle & Co, December 2026.

## The team

369 people, mostly at the Eastgate depot and a few remote. 90 of them have Claude seats. Luis Ferreira runs the department. Ravi Chandran and Chloe Dubois lead the two shifts. Mateo Rossi manages damage claims. We handle complaints and damage claims from clients and the public: about 40 tickets a month reach the leads, and many more are answered on the spot.

## What we do every week

| Work | How often | Who | Time it takes today | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly ticket review: read the week's tickets, check first-reply times against each plan's target, group by theme, chase what's open | Every Monday | Team leads | About 2 hours each | We do it in a spreadsheet. We usually only find an Enterprise miss after the client complains |
| Replying to tickets | Every day | Everyone | 10 to 15 minutes a reply | People copy from the FAQ. The FAQ is out of date in places, so some replies are wrong |
| Follow-up after a client call | 2 or 3 a week | Leads and the claims manager | About 45 minutes each | Promises made on the call get lost. Nobody writes down who owns what |
| Damage claim intake | Every day | Claims manager | 20 minutes a claim | Needs photos, date, time and address. Often has to go back to the client |
| Monthly report for Luis | Once a month | Team leads | Half a day | Built from the weekly reviews |

## One-off work this quarter

- Rewriting the FAQ after the move to Lighthouse Quay
- Choosing a new ticketing tool (the contract renews on 31 December)
- Training two new starters

## Tools

- Help desk: Zendesk. Exports to CSV. Nobody has added its Claude connector yet.
- Team chat: Slack, one channel per shift.
- FAQ and team notes: Notion.
- Email: Microsoft 365.

## What we want

The weekly review done before the Monday stand-up, replies that use the right prices and targets, and no more lost promises after calls.
