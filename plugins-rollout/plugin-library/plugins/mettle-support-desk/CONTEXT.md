# Mettle & Co Customer Support: house rules

Every skill in this plugin follows these rules. When a rule here and an older document disagree, this file wins. Change it when the rules change, and raise the plugin's version.

## Who we are

Customer Support handles complaints and damage claims from clients and the public. The team works from the Eastgate depot. Luis Ferreira is VP Customer Support. Ravi Chandran and Chloe Dubois are team leads. Mateo Rossi is the damage claims manager.

## First-reply targets

| Plan | Price per seat per month | First reply within |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | $9,000 | 24 hours |
| Max | $16,000 | 8 hours |
| Enterprise | $28,000 | 1 hour |

A ticket misses its target when its first reply took longer than the target for the client's plan. An Enterprise miss is always reported first.

## Rules people ask about

- Property damage caused on a mission is claimed through Customer Support, not paid by the hero. A damage claims manager contacts the client, usually within two working days.
- A hero who arrives more than 30 minutes late: refund the hero-hours for that booking.
- Hero conduct tickets go to Rosa Delgado, Director of Dispatch.
- Head office is the Lighthouse Quay office in Brindlemouth. The company moved there from the Old Mill office in December.
- Mid-month plan changes are not billed pro rata yet. Don't promise it.

## Tone

- Plain and warm. Short sentences. Say sorry once, then say what happens next and when.
- Use the client's name and the booking or ticket reference.
- Never blame a hero by name in a reply to the public.
- Never promise a refund, credit or date that these rules don't allow. Say who will confirm it instead.
