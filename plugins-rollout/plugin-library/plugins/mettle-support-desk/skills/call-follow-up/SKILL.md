# Call follow-up

> Read [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) for the house rules and tone, and [CONNECTORS.md](../../CONNECTORS.md) if you see a `~~` placeholder.

## 1. Read the call

Use the transcript or notes you were given. Note who was on the call, the client, their plan, and any ticket numbers mentioned.

## 2. Write four parts

1. **Summary.** Five lines at most: what the client raised, how they feel, and what was agreed.
2. **Commitments.** A table: what was promised, by whom, by when. If no one gave a date, write "no date given" and flag it. A promise without an owner is the most common reason a client calls back.
3. **Follow-up email.** To the client, in the tone in CONTEXT.md. It repeats each commitment with its owner and date. Don't add any promise the call didn't make, and check every promise against the house rules.
4. **Ticket update.** Two or three lines for the ticket's internal notes: what happened on the call and the next step.

## 3. Flag what needs a decision

List anything promised on the call that the house rules don't allow, or that needs someone outside Support (billing, dispatch, engineering). Say who should decide.

If `~~chat` is connected and you were asked to, post the commitments to the team channel. Never post the transcript itself.
